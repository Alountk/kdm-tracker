Lantern Year map

A visual map of one Lantern Year and the 11 Settlement Phase steps in official order. Step classifications follow §5 of the research artifact.

The Lantern Year loop

1

Settlement Phase

Heal returnees, advance the timeline, settlement event, milestones, develop, pick the hunt party — 11 steps.

→
2

Hunt Phase

Choose quarry + level, lay out the hunt board, resolve hunt events space-by-space — or the hunt fails.

→
3

Showdown Phase

Battle to victory or defeat; resolve the Aftermath: rewards, hunt XP, proficiency, deaths.

↺

↺ back to Settlement Phase — the settlement typically advances by one Lantern Year per Settlement Phase.

Hunt fails / all survivors perish "The lantern year is over, and a new Settlement Phase begins." (1.6 rulebook pg. 63) — still loops back to step 1.
Nemesis Encounter year Settlement phase runs normally, but departing survivors skip the Hunt Phase and go straight to the showdown.
Campaign end Game Over milestone at population 0, or the campaign finale monster, ends the loop entirely.

The 11 Settlement Phase steps

Step types (research §5): bookkeeping decision branching — steps 1, 3, 4a, 10 are mostly bookkeeping; 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 mix types; 5/6 contain branching principles.
1

Set Up the Settlement

bookkeeping

Place the settlement board, record sheets, event deck, locations, innovations and principles. No decisions — but the Innovation Deck composition must be exactly correct.

2

Survivors Return

bookkeeping decision

Heal injury levels and remove all tokens from returning survivors (survival/insanity do not refill). Then apply principle/event/innovation return effects — some effects require nominating survivors.

3

Gain Endeavors

bookkeeping

+1 endeavor per returning survivor, plus conditional bonuses from innovations and principles.

4

Update Timeline

bookkeeping decision branching

4a Advance the lantern year (auto) → 4b draw + resolve the settlement event → 4c trigger the story events listed for this year, including any Nemesis Encounter (choose monster + level).

5

Update the Death Count

bookkeeping decision branching

Record every death, including deaths that happen later in the phase (update immediately). The first death-count update ever fires the Principle: Death story event — a permanent one-time choice.

6

Check Milestones

bookkeeping decision branching

Tick reached milestone boxes and resolve their story events (any order if several). Principle choices here are permanent branching: New Life, Society, Hooded Knight.

7

Develop

decision bookkeeping

The core decision step: craft gear, spend endeavors, innovate, build locations — free-form and repeatable. This step must close before you can depart (step 8).

8

Prepare Departing Survivors

decision

Decide continue vs end session; choose the 4 departing survivors; allocate gear and recompute armor points / attribute modifiers. Quarry choice is not here — it opens the Hunt Phase.

9

Special Showdown

branching · loop

If the timeline lists a Special Showdown, departing survivors fight it immediately. Win or lose, the flow loops back to step 5 — no earlier steps repeat.

10

Record & Archive Resources

bookkeeping

Write unspent resources into Settlement Storage and archive the physical cards. This is where Showdown rewards become durable inventory.

11

End Settlement Phase

commit gate bookkeeping

Hard commit point: discard any unspent endeavors, store everything, then proceed to the Hunt Phase (or campaign end).

Flow rules to respect in the UI

Step 9 → loops to step 5

After a Special Showdown, return to Update the Death Count. Steps 1–4 do not repeat: no new endeavors, no new settlement event, no timeline advance.

Step 11 discards endeavors

Unspent endeavors do not carry over. Make step 11 a gated commit: "you are about to lose N endeavors."

No departure before Develop

You cannot prepare departing survivors (step 8) before Develop (step 7) is closed — the rulebook order enforces it.

Milestones can fire mid-phase

Step 6 is the scheduled check, but if a milestone is reached later in the phase, it resolves immediately — the flow must accept interrupts.
